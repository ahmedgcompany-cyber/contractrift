## Evidence-quality caveats

- Most quantitative figures (drift percentages, incident counts, flaky-test costs) come from
  **vendor blogs or secondary citations**. They indicate the problem is widely discussed; they
  do not constitute independent measurement. They are quoted as "reported", never as fact.
- Direct Reddit threads could not be surfaced by the search tool for several queries; Reddit
  pain is therefore represented mostly second-hand (blog authors summarizing forum threads).
- Issue-tracker evidence (S6, S16–S18, S32) is primary evidence of user demand but its volume
  (reaction counts) could not be read from the fetched pages.
