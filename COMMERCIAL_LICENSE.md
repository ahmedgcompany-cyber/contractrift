# Commercial licensing

ContractRift is available under two licenses:

1. **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** ([LICENSE](LICENSE)) — free, including commercial use,
   as long as you comply with the AGPL. In particular, if you modify ContractRift and let users interact
   with it over a network, you must offer them the complete corresponding source of your version.
2. **Commercial license** — a separate agreement with the copyright holder that removes the AGPL
   obligations. Typical reasons:
   - your company policy does not allow AGPL software;
   - you want to embed ContractRift in a proprietary product or offer it as part of a paid service
     without publishing your modifications;
   - you want support, warranties or indemnification.

To ask about a commercial license, open a
[GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/ahmedgcompany-cyber/contractrift/discussions) titled
"Commercial license" (no private details needed at first contact) — the maintainer will follow up privately.

Dual licensing is possible because all contributions are accepted under the terms in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
